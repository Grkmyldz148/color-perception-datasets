Datasets / osa_ucs_1974

OSA Uniform Color Scales — 558-sample committee atlas (CIE specification)

Description

The OSA-UCS atlas: 558 physical colour samples placed at the lattice points of a rhombohedral (cuboctahedral/FCC) close-packed arrangement, designed by the OSA committee so that each interior sample is one EQUAL perceived colour difference from its 12 nearest neighbours. This is the principal suprathreshold uniform-spacing dataset independent of Munsell — the natural held-out anchor for spacing claims. Columns: OSA lattice coordinates (L, j, g) + CIE 1964 10-degree specification (x10, y10, Y10) under D65.
