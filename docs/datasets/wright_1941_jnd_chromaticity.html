Notes

  • Wright reports 35 lines tested by the author; this CSV transcribes the 34 published in Table 1 (plus the spectrum locus) and the 17 transformed in Table 6 (plus its spectrum locus) — some are subsets of the author's full 35-line set with auxiliary information lost in publication.
  • Observer initials RAL and THW were not fully expanded in Wright's published acknowledgment; identified observers include H.V. Walters and L.C. Thomson per Wright's earlier collaborations.
  • The 'spectrum locus' rows are perceptually equal wavelength steps along the spectral curve itself (where l is the wavelength in micrometers, not a fractional position along a straight line).
  • Some cells in Wright's original tables were blank or had auxiliary internal positions (e.g., P1-0.60 has a special l=0.15 point); these are preserved as additional rows in the CSV.
  • RUCS transformation was applied by Wright himself; the canonical CSV preserves both representations for direct comparison.
