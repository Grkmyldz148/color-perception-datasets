Notes

  • Four rows (Moderate blue G, Dark reddish orange G, Light bluish green A, Dark bluish green H — all in phase II) lack fiducial limits because the maximum-likelihood estimator could not converge well. T50 and slope are still reported.
  • Row alignment is based on the documented color center order from the paper (Section 4 of Berns 1991). The 'Moderate bluish green' center has 14 vectors because both phase I and phase II measurements were performed for vectors A-E.
  • Negative S values (probit slope) indicate inconsistent observer responses.
  • P_chisq < 0.05 indicates poor fit of the probit model (e.g., heterogeneous response distributions).
