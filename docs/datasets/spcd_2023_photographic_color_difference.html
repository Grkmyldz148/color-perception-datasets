Notes

  • The actual IMAGE files are NOT in this CSV folder; they require separate download from Google Drive or Baidu Netdisk (large file, ~10+ GB)
  • Without the images, the scores alone are not directly usable for metric evaluation; they serve as labels for an image-pair dataset that the user must download separately
  • Cross-validation splits 1-10 exist in the CD-Flow repo; this folder only has fold 1. Other folds are at https://github.com/haoychen3/CD-Flow/tree/main/data/2 through /10
  • Each pair was rated by multiple observers in the original Wang 2023 experiment; the CSV scores are observer-averaged Δ_V
