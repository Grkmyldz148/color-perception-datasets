Notes

  • OCR of the original paper sometimes read '-' as '2' (e.g., -11.0 → 211.0). All negative-sign anomalies have been manually corrected against the figures/text. Spot-check before high-stakes use.
  • Per-pair raw data (446 pairs × 27 judgments × 9 observers) is NOT in this CSV. The supplementary tables in the paper or contact the authors.
  • Ellipse parameters in Table VII may have slight rounding differences vs the original measurement; the values here are as published.
