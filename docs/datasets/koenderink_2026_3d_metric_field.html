Notes

  • This CSV contains the MEDIAN field across 8 observers (Appendix A2). Per-observer ellipsoid sets are NOT in this CSV. Contact authors or check bioRxiv supplementary if per-observer data is required for inter-observer variability analysis.
  • Scale factors are × 1000 for RGB (range 0-1000) and × 10^7 for Σ entries. Apply inverse scaling before use in formulas.
  • Σ is RAW symmetric positive-definite matrix; to obtain the standard ellipsoid representation use the eigendecomposition: Σ = U D U^T where D contains squared semi-axes.
  • Display assumed γ-encoded standard sRGB (per paper Appendix A1); for CIELAB or other space comparisons, transform RGB → display luminance → XYZ → target space.
