Datasets / fairchild_pirrotta_1991

Fairchild & Pirrotta (1991) chromatic-lightness (Helmholtz-Kohlrausch) object-colour data

Description

The classic OBJECT-colour Helmholtz-Kohlrausch dataset: 11 observers matched the perceived lightness of a variable achromatic sample to each of 36 chromatic Munsell papers (4 hues 5R/5Y/2.5G/5PB x 3 values x 3 chroma levels). The "observed_lightness" column is the mean L* of the equal-lightness achromatic match = the H-K-corrected perceptual lightness. Chromatic samples appear LIGHTER than their measured L* by an amount that grows with chroma (observed - L* rises from ~3 at C*~10 to ~11 at C*~71) — the H-K effect on surface colours. The reference for CIELAB L* corrections (the L** metric).
