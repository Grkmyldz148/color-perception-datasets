Datasets / Fairchild_Johnson_1999

Fairchild & Johnson (1999) cross-media corresponding colors (printed / D65-CRT / D50-CRT)

Cross-media color reproduction matches: 110 colors from 6 natural scenes (barn 24, colorchecker 25, fruit 19, golfer 25, noidea 1, pinklady 16), each given as a printed ↔ D65-CRT ↔ D50-CRT corresponding-color triple (3 media × 110 colors = 330 rows).

corresponding-colors

Description

Cross-media appearance-reproduction experiment by Fairchild & Johnson (1999): observers matched colors across printed hardcopy and CRT display under D65 and D50 states, producing corresponding-color triples per scene color. The hue_id column holds SCENE names (barn, colorchecker, fruit, golfer, noidea, pinklady), not hue loci. Useful as a corresponding-colors / chromatic-adaptation judge for cross-media reproduction.

Notes

  • ⚠ SCHEMA DÜZELTMESİ (2026-06-12): Bu set constant-hue verisi DEĞİLDİR. coloria-dev kaynak dosyasının başlığı iki makaleyi karıştırır — abstract Ebner&Fairchild 1998 SPIE hue-yüzeyleri (306×15, o veri havuzda ebner_fairchild olarak ZATEN var), veri ise Fairchild&Johnson 1999 CR&A cross-media reproduction deneyi (printed ↔ D65-CRT ↔ D50-CRT eşleşmeleri). hue_id kolonu sahne adıdır, lokus değil. Constant-hue yargıcı olarak KULLANILAMAZ (G1 G0-c2 verdikti, color-space/research/fortress/G1_G0C2_VERDICT.md); corresponding-colors / adaptasyon yargıcı olarak kullanılabilir (printed↔CRT üçlüleri).
  • 2026-07-08 audit: 330 rows verified = 3 media × 110 colors, every color id complete as a printed/d65crt/d50crt triple; 15 rows at L=100 with nonzero a,b are printed-medium white patches (media-relative colorimetry). The 15 rows at L=100 are plausible, not errors.
  • The raw YAML's top-level key is the medium (e.g. `printed`); inner keys are scene names — refer to the paper for details.
  • Per-chip observer counts and inter-observer variability are summarized in the paper but not preserved in the raw YAML.
